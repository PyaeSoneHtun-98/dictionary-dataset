# Independent idiom review

Reviewed all 545 canonical entries, all stored forms, and all Burmese glosses in the 545-entry snapshot of `idioms.json`. Source SHA-256: `b6ba07a0d7132bae94700f651c0c3abd550f4fcd45423322a2dbc827943893ec`.

Findings: 22 entries (17 reject; 5 replace). The remaining 523 entries have no material finding. Findings are keyed by canonical phrase so they remain applicable if the author prunes the source. The source draft and frozen assets were not edited.

Checks covered genuine lexical units, mandatory intervening objects, actual contiguous surfaces, irregular verb forms, possessive and plural forms, semantic match, and natural independently authored Burmese. An additional punctuation-equivalence comparison against every frozen canonical/form found no frozen equivalent after replacing apostrophes and hyphens with spaces.

Most rejections are bare active patterns with a missing mandatory object. Existing passive participles were not used to rescue those canonical patterns, following the coordinator's conservative contiguous-matching policy. Genuine object-after-phrase constructions such as `give short shrift`, `give a wide berth to`, and `throw cold water on` were retained. Normal possessive-object idioms were also retained. No automated conjugation was trusted as linguistic evidence.

Limitations: this is a linguistic/editorial review, not a subtitle-frequency corpus audit. A rejected object-omitting active head can have valid passive occurrences. A legitimate dated or regional idiom was not rejected solely for being dated or regional. Structural collision validation and selection among overlaps with other new drafts remain with the coordinator. No native-speaker panel was available; Burmese was reviewed with language understanding.
