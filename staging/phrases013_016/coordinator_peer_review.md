# Independent coordinator draft review

Reviewed all 240 coordinator entries, including every stored form, Burmese meaning, and type. Findings: 6 rejections and 19 replacements; 215 entries have no requested change. Replacement objects are full entries and can be applied by the coordinator. The source draft and all frozen/infrastructure files were left unchanged.

The review focuses on semantic accuracy, natural Burmese, genuine new expressions, conventional contiguous constructions, and forms. Five low-priority rejections are editorial judgments, not claims that the expressions are invalid English. The sixth rejection is a punctuation-only variant of a frozen phrase. Legitimate British English and standard optional-initial-as forms were not rejected merely for being regional or variants.

Most supplied forms are sound. Suggested plural hearts/belts and reflexive variants increase useful matching; existing collective or singular-they readings are retained rather than declared wrong. No unreviewed suffix generation was used. Final combined-batch collision filtering and corpus-frequency measurement remain the coordinator's responsibilities; this is a linguistic peer review, not a native-speaker panel or empirical subtitle-frequency study.

- [Collins: up the wall](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/up-the-wall) — supports the standalone idiom, so no incompleteness rejection was made.
