# Coordinator draft and selection notes

The coordinator authored 240 new phrase entries in `coordinator.psv`, then compiled them to `coordinator.json`. Burmese glosses were independently written from language understanding. Initial candidates and unpublished alternatives are in `coordinator_candidates.txt`; candidate presence does not imply acceptance.

All 240 canonical strings were absent from the frozen 7,827-key namespace. The draft initially contained 194 useful forms, including manually selected tense forms, standard optional-initial-as simile forms, and possessive surface variations. A second agent reviewed every entry and form; the final selection applies that review rather than trusting transformations without review. The coordinator removed a one-token hyphenated form because runtime phrase forms require 2–5 tokens.

Selection is a qualitative editorial judgment about subtitle relevance, not a measured claim about subtitle-corpus frequency. Native Burmese sign-off and Windows application QA have not been performed.
