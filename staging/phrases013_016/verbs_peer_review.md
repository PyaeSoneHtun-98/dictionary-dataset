# Independent verbs-pool peer review

Reviewed all 452 entries in verbs.json, including all Burmese meanings and every stored form. The original file was not edited. The JSON records are concrete replacements/rejections for coordinator review; the source pool remains unchanged.

Most phrasal-verb meanings and conjugations are sound. Material findings concern two incomplete mandatory-object canonical patterns, one ordinary compositional collocation, wording issues in four Burmese glosses, two type corrections, noun-versus-verb wording for rubber stamp, partner terminology, and useful countable noun plurals. Three low-priority specialist entries and one redundant noun elaboration are recommended for exclusion from this particular common-idiom selection; this does not claim they are invalid English.

All proposed replacement entries pass schema/token/normalization/Burmese-ASCII checks and their keys avoid the frozen 7,827-key index. The coordinator must still check replacement forms against all other agents' candidates and final chosen entries. I did not perform a corpus frequency study or audit legal/military policy as professional guidance; glosses explain ordinary subtitle meanings, and selected technical readings remain intentionally concise.
